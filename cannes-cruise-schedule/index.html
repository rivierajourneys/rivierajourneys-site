Schedule

Cannes is a tender port on the western French Riviera. Ships anchor in the Bay of Cannes and tender passengers ashore to the old port, near the Palais des Festivals. From Cannes, Antibes and Saint-Paul-de-Vence are twenty minutes east. Nice is thirty minutes by motorway. Eze and Monaco are forty-five minutes along the coast. Grasse, the perfume capital, is thirty minutes into the hills above. In 2026, 68 ships are scheduled to call at Cannes, from SeaDream yachts with 112 guests to MSC Grandiosa with nearly six thousand. This is the complete schedule.

Port guide.
What you need to know.

Is Cannes a tender port?

Yes. The old port is too small for large ships. They anchor in the Bay of Cannes and tender passengers ashore to the old port. Tenders dock near Quai Max Laubeuf (currently under renovation, completion expected 2027).

Where do you meet us?

At the tender landing near the old port, on boulevard Jean Hibert. We are there with a name board before your tender docks. The car is within a minute.

How long do we have ashore?

Most Cannes calls give 8 to 10 hours. Tell us your ship name and departure time — we plan an itinerary that gets you back before the last tender.

What can we see from Cannes?

Antibes and Saint-Paul-de-Vence are 20 minutes east. Nice is 30 minutes. Eze and Monaco are 45 minutes. Grasse is 30 minutes into the hills. Provence wine country about an hour.

How busy is Cannes as a cruise port?

About 68 arrivals in 2026. MSC Grandiosa calls weekly in summer. Busiest months: May, July, August.

Is there a limit on ship size?

Since January 2026, Cannes restricts cruise ships carrying more than 1,000 passengers from the old port. Larger ships anchor in the bay and tender ashore.
