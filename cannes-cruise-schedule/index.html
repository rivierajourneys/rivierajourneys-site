Schedule

Cannes is a tender port. Cruise ships anchor in the bay of La Croisette and tender boats bring passengers ashore to Quai Laubeuf, at the western end of the Old Port. From the pier you can walk to the Palais des Festivals in two minutes, or step into our car for the rest of the Riviera. Antibes is fifteen minutes east. Saint-Paul-de-Vence and Grasse are thirty minutes north. Nice is thirty minutes east. Monaco and Eze are fifty minutes east. Saint-Tropez is around an hour and a quarter west. Below is the rolling schedule of every scheduled call — from luxury yachts to the largest ships permitted under the 1,000-passenger landing cap introduced in January 2026. Your day ashore

The earlier your tender, the more the Riviera opens up.

Cannes has always been a tender port: your ship anchors in the bay and tenders land at quai Laubeuf on the Vieux Port, where we meet you with a name board. A private excursion is €800 per vehicle for up to seven guests, built around your tender window. What fits depends on when you land:

Ashore by 10:00
All three routes fit at full length — Nice, Eze and Monaco along the coast, Antibes and Saint-Paul-de-Vence close to the ship, or inland to Grasse and Gourdon.
Ashore from 11:00
The eastern route to Nice, Eze and Monaco is still possible in full if your last tender is after six in the evening. If it is earlier, one stop has to go — usually Nice. Antibes and Saint-Paul, or Grasse on its own, make the better day.
The return
We plan to have you back at quai Laubeuf thirty to forty-five minutes before the last tender. Tell us your ship and we match the route to the hours you have.

Port guide.
What you need to know.

Is Cannes a tender port?

Yes. Cannes is too small to dock large cruise ships, so vessels anchor in the bay and tender boats bring passengers ashore to Quai Laubeuf in the Old Port. The tender ride takes 10 to 15 minutes. We meet you at the tender landing with a name board.

Where do you meet us?

At Quai Laubeuf — the tender landing pier in Cannes Old Port, at the western end of the Croisette. We are there before your tender docks. The car is a minute’s walk from the pier.

How long do we have ashore?

It depends on your ship. Most Cannes calls give 7 to 10 hours ashore. Tell us your ship name and we confirm the shore window and recommend an itinerary.

What can we see from Cannes?

Antibes is 15 minutes east. Saint-Paul-de-Vence and Grasse are 30 minutes north. Nice is 30 minutes east. Monaco and Eze are 50 minutes east. Saint-Tropez is around an hour and a quarter west when traffic allows.

Can the tender be delayed?

Yes. Tender delays at Cannes happen, especially during the Film Festival or when MSC Grandiosa calls. We wait at no extra charge. If your shore time is reduced, we shorten the itinerary.

How often is the schedule updated?

Once a week. The schedule is pulled automatically from publicly available cruise line itineraries every Sunday. If your ship is not listed, send us the name on WhatsApp and we will check directly.
