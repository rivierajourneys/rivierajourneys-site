An introduction to a place

The French and Italian Riviera has been visited by millions and genuinely understood by very few. Most people arrive, photograph the postcard, and leave with a collection of images that look exactly like everyone else's. We do something different: we show you the Riviera as it is actually lived — the roads that don't appear on tour maps, the villages that reward patience, the light at a particular hour on a particular stretch of coastline that no organised excursion will get you to on time.

Riviera Journeys is a private touring service based in Cannes. We run day tours, multi-day itineraries, and shore excursions for cruise guests across the French and Italian Riviera, and airport and port transfers — all in a premium Mercedes V-Class. The difference is not the vehicle. It is the knowledge of place that comes from living here.

Before You Book

Questions & answers.

Our full-day private tours are priced at €800 per vehicle — not per person — for up to seven guests. Transfers from Nice Côte d'Azur Airport begin at €120. Shore excursions from Villefranche start from €240. There are no hidden costs: fuel, parking, and waiting time are included.

May we adjust the itinerary to suit our own interests?

Entirely. Every route we offer is a suggestion, not a schedule. If you would prefer to spend longer in Eze and skip Monaco, or to find a particular village in the arrière-pays, or simply to follow the Corniche until the light is right — we adjust accordingly. Please mention your preferences when booking and we shall design the day around them.

How long is a full-day tour, precisely?

A full day typically runs seven to eight hours from departure to return. We collect you at your hotel, port, or agreed meeting point at 09:00–09:30 and return you by late afternoon — before the roads acquire their evening character. The precise schedule is agreed when booking and can accommodate early cruise departures or late hotel check-outs.

Our party numbers more than seven — can you accommodate us?

The Mercedes V-Class seats seven passengers comfortably. For larger parties, we arrange two vehicles travelling together on the same route — your group remains together throughout the day. The price per vehicle is identical. Please let us know the size of your party when enquiring.

What is the best shore excursion from Villefranche?

Nice, Eze and Monaco together make the most complete day ashore — Nice old town in the morning, the village of Eze at the right hour, then Monaco with time for the quarter above the port rather than merely the Casino forecourt. The route is adjusted to your ship's departure time, so you are back at the tender pier comfortably before she weighs anchor.

Is it possible to be collected from Nice airport and taken directly to Cannes?

Yes — airport transfers are among our most frequently requested services. We meet you in the arrivals hall with a name board, assist with luggage, and have you in Cannes within forty minutes under normal conditions. The rate is fixed regardless of traffic or flight delays. Transfers to Monaco, Antibes, and elsewhere on the Riviera are equally available.

Do you conduct tours in languages other than English?

Our tours are conducted in English, Russian and French. If your party includes guests who would be more comfortable in French or Russian, we are entirely at ease switching between the two throughout the day.

Do you offer tours in winter and off-season?

We do — and we would argue the Riviera is at its most interesting between October and April. The truffle markets open in November, the ski resorts above Nice are accessible by mid-December, and the light on the coast is exceptional all winter. Our off-season programme includes gastronomy days, snow and sea combinations, and cultural visits to places that are impossible to appreciate properly in summer.

We are attending MIPIM — can you arrange transfers for our entire delegation?

Yes, and this is something we do well. For MIPIM, Cannes Lions, the Festival, and ILTM, we coordinate arrival and departure transfers for groups of any size, working with our network of vetted VTC partners. Fixed rates are agreed in advance, all vehicles are premium Mercedes, and you receive a single point of contact for the entire week. We recommend confirming arrangements at least six weeks before the event.

We have two weeks on the Riviera and do not know where to start. Can you help us plan the stay?

This is precisely what our Slow Travel Consultation is for. In ninety minutes — by video or in person in Cannes — we design your stay from the ground up: where to base yourself and why, what to do on each day in what order, which places reward patience and which are better avoided, and where to eat without being steered toward the obvious. You leave with a personalised itinerary PDF. The consultation costs €250 and can be booked through the site.

How far in advance ought one to book?

We recommend booking at least a week ahead, particularly between May and September when the Riviera is at its most sought-after. That said, we do our best to accommodate last-minute requests — if the date is available, the tour will happen. Contact us directly by email or WhatsApp and we will respond within two hours.
