The French and Italian Riviera has been visited by millions and genuinely understood by very few. We do something different. We show the Riviera as it is actually lived — the roads that don’t appear on tour maps, the villages that reward patience, the light at a particular hour on a particular stretch of coastline. There are no group departures and there will not be. Each day, each route, each transfer is arranged for one party at a time. The Mercedes seats up to seven comfortably. The roads we drive include the three Corniches above Monaco, the back routes through the arrière-pays of Grasse, the coastal road into Italy as far as Sanremo or Cinque Terre, and the perched villages above the Loup gorge that nine in ten visitors will never reach.

Day tours from €800. Shore excursions for cruise guests at Cannes and Villefranche. Multi-day journeys through France and Italy. Fourteen transfer routes from Nice Côte d’Azur Airport, beginning at €140. Corporate transfers during the festivals at the Palais. All in a single Mercedes V-Class, driven by someone who lives here.

Before You Book

Questions and answers

How much does a private day tour from Cannes cost?
Our full-day private tours are priced at €800 per vehicle — not per person — for up to seven guests. Transfers from Nice Côte d’Azur Airport begin at €140. Shore excursions from Villefranche from €240. There are no hidden costs: fuel, parking, and waiting time are included.
May we adjust the itinerary to suit our own interests?
Entirely. Every route we offer is a suggestion, not a schedule. If you would prefer to spend longer in Eze and skip Monaco, or to find a particular village in the arrière-pays, or simply to follow the Corniche until the light is right — we adjust accordingly.
How long is a full-day tour, precisely?
A full day typically runs seven to eight hours from departure to return. We collect you at your hotel, port, or agreed meeting point at 09:00–09:30 and return you by late afternoon — before the roads acquire their evening character.
Our party numbers more than seven — can you accommodate us?
The Mercedes V-Class seats seven passengers comfortably. For larger parties, we arrange two vehicles travelling together on the same route — your group remains together throughout the day. The price per vehicle is identical.
What is the best shore excursion from Villefranche?
Nice, Eze and Monaco together make the most complete day ashore — Nice old town in the morning, the village of Eze at the right hour, then Monaco with time for the quarter above the port rather than merely the Casino forecourt. The route is adjusted to your ship’s departure time.
Is it possible to be collected from Nice airport?
Yes — airport transfers are among our most frequently requested services. We meet you in the arrivals hall with a name board, assist with luggage, and have you in Cannes within forty minutes under normal conditions. The rate is fixed regardless of traffic or flight delays, and the same fixed-price principle applies to all transfer routes we operate.
Do you offer multi-day journeys into Italy?
Yes — the most established route is Cannes to Florence over five to seven days, via Genova, Cinque Terre and the Tuscan hills. We arrange overnight accommodation, drive routes that reward driving, and adjust the pace to the party. Pricing is on enquiry, as it depends on dates and hotels chosen.
