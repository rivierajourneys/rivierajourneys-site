Every operator on the Riviera will drive you to Monaco. Most of them will show you the Casino, the palace, the Grand Prix circuit, and bring you home with a photograph that looks exactly like everyone else’s. What none of them will mention — because none of them have bothered to find out — is who actually built the Casino, where the money came from, and why a small provincial town with no industry and no particular reason to exist became the most expensive square kilometre on earth. That story is considerably more interesting than the building itself.

The same applies to every stop on every route we drive. You can see Eze as a hilltop village with a view. Or you can understand why it was built on that particular hilltop, and by whom, and what happened there that still shapes the road you drove up. We are not especially interested in showing you things. We are interested in making sure you understand the places you stand in.

Questions. Straight answers.

Where do you pick up in Cannes?

From your hotel, residence or any address in Cannes. Most pick-ups are along the Croisette, at the old port, or near the Palais des Festivals. We also collect from Antibes, Juan-les-Pins and Mougins.

How long is a day tour?

A full day is typically eight to nine hours, departing between 8:30 and 9:30. Half-day tours — Grasse and the hilltop villages, for example — run four to five hours. We adjust to your preferences.

How much does a private tour cost?

€800 per vehicle for standard routes — up to seven passengers. Fuel, tolls, parking and the vehicle for the entire day are included. No hidden costs.

Can we change the route during the tour?

Yes. Every route is a framework, not a fixed schedule. Skip a stop and spend longer somewhere else. Add a detour to a village market. Stop for a longer lunch. The best days are the ones that follow what interests you.

What vehicle do you use?

A Mercedes V-Class — seven seats, air-conditioned, tinted windows, ample luggage space. Comfortable for a full day on Riviera roads. One vehicle, one party — no strangers, no group tours.
