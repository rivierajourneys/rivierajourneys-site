Mise à disposition

The service between
a transfer and a tour.

A transfer takes you from one address to another. A day tour follows a route. Between the two sits the service most of our guests actually end up booking: a car and driver held at your disposal, by the hour or for the day, with no route at all. You decide where the day goes — and change your mind as it goes. The driver waits, the boot carries the shopping, the plan is whatever the plan becomes.

The rate is €100 per hour with a three-hour minimum, and a full day of up to eight hours is capped at €800 — the same per-vehicle price as our day tours. The figure covers everything: the Mercedes V-Class, the English-speaking driver, fuel, motorway tolls, parking, and all the waiting a real day involves. Up to seven passengers travel for the same price, so a family or two couples share one figure, not four fares.

We work from Cannes and serve the whole coast — Nice, Antibes, Saint-Tropez, Grasse, Villefranche, Menton, and Monaco for drop-offs and through-days. One honest limit: under our French VTC licence we cannot collect passengers inside the Principality, so a Monaco evening begins and ends on the French side of the border. We say so here because you would find out anyway.

The most common booking of all is a transfer that becomes a day: an airport arrival, then the same car and driver kept for the days that follow.

Questions

The ones people
actually ask.

How much does a private chauffeur cost on the French Riviera?

€100 per hour per vehicle, with a minimum booking of three hours (€300). A full day of up to eight hours is €800 — the same per-vehicle price as our day tours. The rate covers the Mercedes V-Class, the driver, fuel, waiting time, motorway tolls and parking, for up to seven passengers. There are no additions at the end of the day.

What is the minimum booking?

Three hours, €300. For a single journey from A to B with no stops, a fixed-price transfer is usually better value — Nice Airport to Cannes is €130, for example. The hourly service exists for days with several stops, open timing, or a car that stays with you between them.

How does a full day work?

A full day is up to eight hours at €800 — the day rate simply caps the hourly rate, so you never pay more than €800 for a standard day. Beyond eight hours, each additional hour is €100. For several consecutive days with the same driver, we quote a package rather than a sum of day rates.

Does waiting time cost extra?

No. Waiting is the service. While you are at lunch, in a meeting, in a fitting room or on a yacht, the car and driver stay at your disposal within the booked window. There are no waiting fees and no per-stop charges — the clock is the only meter.

Can you collect us in Monaco?

No. We operate under a French VTC licence, which does not permit collecting passengers inside the Principality. Drop-offs in Monaco are no problem, and a day that begins in France, enters Monaco and leaves with the same passengers is not affected — that is how most Monaco evenings and casino dinners work with us.

Should I book a chauffeur by the hour or a fixed-price transfer?

A clean A-to-B journey at a known time is a transfer — fixed price, simpler, usually cheaper. Book the hourly service when the day has moving parts: several addresses, shopping, a lunch of unknown length, an evening that may run late, or a schedule you want to keep flexible. If you describe the day, we will honestly recommend whichever costs you less.

What do guests typically use the hourly service for?

A shore day from a cruise call, assembled around your own timing. Shopping on the Croisette or in Monte-Carlo with the car holding your purchases. An evening out — dinner in Monaco or Saint-Paul-de-Vence with the car waiting. Yacht guests ashore for the day. Property viewings. A business day across Cannes, Nice and Sophia Antipolis.

Can the same driver stay with us for several days?

Yes, subject to availability — the most common booking of all is an airport transfer that becomes two or three days with the same car and driver. If your dates are fixed, book early; there is one vehicle and the diary fills first in summer. Multi-day engagements are quoted as a package.
