# GBP Content Pack — Riviera Journeys
*Prepared 27 April 2026*

This pack contains four pieces of content for the Google Business Profile, in the order to apply them. All copy is in the brand's editorial voice (dry, specific, first-person plural, British English). No superlatives, no "premium / luxury / bespoke", no emoji, no exclamation marks.

---

## 1 · Business description (paste into GBP)

**Where to put it:** GBP dashboard → "Edit profile" → "About" → "Business description". Maximum 750 characters; the version below is 737.

```
Riviera Journeys is a private tour and transfer bureau on the Côte d'Azur, based in Cannes. We arrange day tours from Cannes and Nice, shore excursions from the cruise ports at Villefranche and Cannes, airport transfers from Nice, and multi-day journeys through France and Italy. The vehicle is a Mercedes V-Class, up to seven passengers. We work with one vehicle and one set of guests at a time, which means a route can be held loosely and rearranged around weather, light, market days, and what you actually want to see. The photographs on rivierajourneys.fr are taken by us, in the field. Languages: English, French, Russian. Fixed prices, agreed at booking.
```

**Why this version:**
- First sentence states the entity, location and what it is — exactly what Google's local algorithm and AI Overviews need.
- "Cruise ports at Villefranche and Cannes" — captures specific shore-excursion intent.
- "One vehicle and one set of guests at a time" is the differentiator vs. Chabé / French Riviera Drivers (large fleets).
- "Photographs taken by us, in the field" — preserves the authorial differentiator without naming the operator. Sets RJ apart from operators using stock photography, without turning the GBP into a personal page.
- "Riviera Journeys is a private tour and transfer bureau" — strong entity signal that disambiguates from Riviera Travel (UK river cruises).

## 3 · GBP Posts — pack of 4 (one per week through May)

Each post should be published as a "What's new" update in GBP. **Don't add a CTA button** — the editorial voice doesn't suit "Book now". Just text + photo.

### Post 1 — Week of 28 April

**Photo:** A mid-morning shot of the Croisette or Cannes old port. Daylight. No people in foreground.

**Text (286 characters):**
```
The cruise season opens this week in Villefranche. Eight ships scheduled in May — Silversea, Oceania, Regent Seven Seas. We pick up at the tender pier on quai Courbet, with a name board, before your tender docks. Bay of Villefranche to Eze village is twenty minutes uphill on the Moyenne Corniche.
```

### Post 2 — Week of 5 May

**Photo:** Fragonard storefront in Grasse, or a perfumery counter shot, or lavender if you have it.

**Text (273 characters):**
```
Grasse is fifteen minutes inland from Cannes by motorway, and forty by the Route Napoleon if you prefer the long way. Fragonard's factory tour is free and ends in their boutique, which is the practical answer to "what should I bring back from the South of France". We wait outside.
```

### Post 3 — Week of 12 May

**Photo:** Dolceacqua bridge or the medieval arch entry into Apricale (Italian Riviera shots, since cross-border is your differentiator).

**Text (268 characters):**
```
Forty minutes east of Menton you cross into Italy and the road softens. Dolceacqua, Apricale, Pigna — three medieval villages over an afternoon, lunch in one of them. Different mountain, different wine, different language. The MIPIM and Cannes Lions weeks are fully booked; standard days remain.
```

### Post 4 — Week of 19 May

**Photo:** Cannes Lions credentials hanging at the Palais, or a long-lens shot of the Croisette during festival, or simply the Mercedes parked at the Palais entrance.

**Text (294 characters):**
```
Cannes Lions runs 16–20 June this year. We hold a vehicle and driver for the full week — early morning client breakfasts, terrace lunches at the Carlton or Martinez, dinners that run late, the unscheduled Antibes detour. Hourly chauffeur from €100 with a three-hour minimum. Day rate €1,000.
```

## 4 · Q&A pre-fill (5 questions)

GBP allows the business owner to "ask and answer" their own questions, which appear with a verified-by-owner badge. These are evergreen, written in the same voice. **Where:** GBP dashboard → "Q&A" → "Ask a question" — but pose it from a separate Google account if you have one (or a friend's), then answer from the verified owner account.

**Q1.** *How much does a private day tour cost?*
> €800 per vehicle for up to seven guests. Fixed at booking, agreed in writing. Entrance fees, lunches and tips are not included; we reserve restaurants ahead if you'd like.

**Q2.** *Where do you pick up cruise passengers in Villefranche?*
> At quai de l'Amiral Courbet, the tender landing in the village. We are there with a name board before your tender docks. The car is thirty seconds from the pier.

**Q3.** *Do you speak English?*
> Yes — English, French and Russian.

**Q4.** *Can we change the route on the day?*
> Entirely. Routes are starting points. We adjust to weather, light, hunger, and what you find interesting once you are out.

**Q5.** *How far in advance should we book?*
> A week is comfortable. The festival weeks (MIPIM in March, Lions in June, Film Festival in May, ILTM in December) book out months ahead. Off-season days are usually open last-minute.

## How to publish — step by step

1. **GBP description:** open GBP dashboard → Edit profile → About → Business description → paste the text in §1 → Save. Takes 1 minute.

2. **Categories:** Edit profile → Business category → check primary is "Sightseeing tour agency" → review additional categories → add any missing from §2 list. 2 minutes.

3. **Q&A:** ask and answer the 5 questions from §4 over a span of 3–5 days (don't post all at once — Google deprioritises self-Q&A clusters). 5 minutes total.

4. **Posts:** publish Post 1 this week. Then one per week for 4 weeks. Set a calendar reminder. Each post takes 2 minutes.

**Total your time across the month:** ~25 minutes.

**What you should see by end of May:**
- Profile completeness near 100% (Google will say so in the dashboard).
- Posts cycle keeps the profile "active" — Google's local algorithm rewards weekly activity.
- Q&A populated answers replace the empty "Ask a question" prompt that currently looks dead.
- Anyone who searches "private driver Cannes" and then taps your GBP gets a more useful first impression.
