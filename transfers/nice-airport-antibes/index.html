Nice Airport → Cannes

The transfer most
Riviera visitors need first.

Nice Côte d'Azur Airport is the arrival point for the vast majority of visitors to the French Riviera. Saint-Tropez is 100 kilometres southwest — further than any other standard Riviera transfer. The route takes the A8 to Le Muy, then D25 and D559 coast road into Saint-Tropez. Under normal conditions: 90 minutes. In July and August, particularly on Friday afternoons: 2.5 to 3 hours or more. The D559 is the only road in. There is no alternative.

We meet you in arrivals — in the hall, with a name board, before you reach the taxi queue outside. The vehicle is a Mercedes V-Class with space for 7 passengers and all their luggage. The price is fixed, confirmed before you travel, and does not change with traffic or time of day. The return leg to Nice Airport for your departure flight is available at the same rate. We advise on a specific departure time when you book — summer departures need more planning than arrivals.

We also serve the Nice business aviation terminal (ACAM) for private jet arrivals — please mention this when booking. For parties of 8 or more, we arrange two vehicles at the same per-vehicle rate.

The D559 into Saint-Tropez is the same road whether you arrive at noon or midnight. In summer, we plan for it. In winter, it is a different place entirely.

Nice Côte d'Azur Airport — NCE

Commercial terminal arrivals

Nice NCE has two terminals. Both handle a mix of international and European flights — there is no strict geographic split. Emirates, for example, operates from Terminal 2. The correct terminal depends on your airline and flight, not your origin.

Nice Business Aviation — ACAM

Private jet terminal

The ACAM business aviation terminal is a separate facility at Nice Airport, used by private jet arrivals. It is not connected to the commercial terminals. If you are arriving by private aircraft, please specify this when booking — the meeting point and parking arrangements differ.

Questions

The ones people
actually ask.

How much does a private transfer from Nice Airport to Saint-Tropez cost?

From €140 per vehicle — for up to 7 passengers, tolls and airport parking included. The figure confirmed at booking is the figure paid at the door. There are no additions at the end of the journey.

How long does the transfer from Nice Airport to Saint-Tropez take?

Approximately 90 minutes in normal conditions — A8 to Le Muy, D25 then D559 into Saint-Tropez. In July and August, particularly on Friday afternoons and Saturday mornings, allow 2.5 to 3 hours. The D559 is a single-carriageway coast road with no alternative route; in peak season it fills completely. We always advise on a specific departure time when you book.

Where exactly do you meet passengers at Nice Airport?

In the arrivals hall, name board at the barrier past customs — before you reach the exit doors and the taxi queue outside. We track your flight from departure and know your terminal automatically. You do not need to confirm the terminal when booking.

Do you monitor flight arrivals?

Yes. Every airport transfer includes real-time flight tracking from the moment your flight departs. If it is delayed, the pickup time adjusts automatically. You do not need to call or message — the driver is already tracking the board. This is standard on all bookings, not an extra service.

Do you serve the Nice business aviation terminal?

Terminal 1 handles most long-haul, transatlantic, and non-Schengen arrivals. Terminal 2 handles most European, domestic French, and low-cost carrier arrivals. The two terminals are 5 minutes apart by shuttle and are not within walking distance. Check your boarding pass or airline confirmation for the terminal, and let us know when booking.

Yes. We serve the ACAM business aviation terminal at Nice Airport, which is a separate facility used by private jet arrivals. If you are arriving by private aircraft, please mention this when booking — the meeting point and parking arrangements differ from a commercial arrival.

Can I book a return transfer from Saint-Tropez to Nice Airport?

Yes, at the same rate. We strongly recommend booking both directions together — particularly in summer, when departure timing from Saint-Tropez is critical. For a morning flight, we sometimes advise leaving Saint-Tropez the evening before and overnighting near Nice. We discuss this honestly when you book.

Is a private transfer to Saint-Tropez more expensive than a taxi?

A private transfer to Saint-Tropez is substantially different from a taxi. The journey is 100 kilometres and takes 90 minutes to 3 hours depending on season. A taxi from Nice Airport to Saint-Tropez at metered rates in summer traffic will often cost more than €375 — and the price is not known in advance. A private transfer gives you a fixed figure, a confirmed vehicle for up to 7 passengers, and a driver who knows the route and the conditions. For groups, the per-head cost is significantly lower than individual taxis.
