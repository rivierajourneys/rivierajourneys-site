Antibes

Old town, Cap and Juan-les-Pins.
We know the way.

Questions

The ones people
actually ask.

How much does the transfer from Cannes to Antibes cost?

€90 per vehicle for up to 7 passengers. Tolls included. Cap d'Antibes is the same rate. Price confirmed at booking is price paid.

How long does the drive take?

20–25 minutes in normal conditions. In peak summer on the coastal road, allow 35–40 minutes.

Can you deliver to Cap d'Antibes hotels and villas?

Yes. We deliver to Hôtel du Cap-Eden-Roc, Villa Eilenroc, and any private estate on the Cap. Give us the full address and gate code at booking.

Do you serve Juan-les-Pins?

Yes. Same rate as Antibes — it is the same distance from Cannes.

Can I book a return from Antibes to Cannes?

Yes, at €90. We recommend booking both legs together.

Is there a connection to Nice Airport from Antibes?

Yes. We run Antibes → Nice Airport from €140. If you need Cannes → Antibes → Nice Airport in one journey, we quote a combined fixed price.
