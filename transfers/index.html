Questions

The ones people actually ask.

How much does a private transfer from Nice Airport to Cannes cost?
From €140 per vehicle to Cannes, €150 to Monaco, €390 to Saint-Tropez. All prices are fixed, per vehicle, for up to 7 passengers — tolls and airport parking included. A night surcharge applies from 22:00 to 06:00. The figure confirmed at booking is the figure paid at the door.
Is a private transfer more expensive than a taxi from Nice Airport?
A private transfer is a different service from a taxi. The vehicle is a Mercedes V-Class with space for 7 passengers and all their luggage. The driver is English-speaking and pre-booked. The price is confirmed in writing before the journey — it does not change with traffic, time of day, or the number of people in the vehicle. A taxi can be hailed in the moment; a private transfer is arranged in advance, with a named driver and a fixed figure. For groups travelling together, for airport pickups where timing matters, and for journeys where the price needs to be known before departure, the two are not really comparable.
Do you monitor flight arrivals?
Yes. Every airport transfer includes real-time flight tracking from the moment the flight departs. If it is delayed, the pickup time adjusts automatically. You do not need to call or message — the driver is already tracking the board. This is standard on all bookings, not an extra service.
Can I book a return transfer from Cannes to Nice Airport?
Yes. Return and departure transfers from Cannes, Monaco, Antibes, and Saint-Tropez to Nice NCE are available at the same rates. We recommend booking both directions together so the departure leg is confirmed before you arrive.
What is included in the transfer price?
The Mercedes V-Class, up to 7 passengers, all luggage, motorway tolls, airport parking, and a professional English-speaking driver. Child seats on request at no charge. The figure quoted is the figure paid — no additions at the door, no waiting-time surcharge within reason.
Do you cover the Cannes Film Festival, MIPIM, and Monaco Grand Prix?
Yes. Standard rates apply during all events. Traffic during Film Festival, MIPIM, and Grand Prix week can be severe — pickup times are planned with this in mind. Availability is limited during these periods; book early. For corporate groups needing multiple vehicles across an event week, see our Corporate & Events section.
Do you cover the Italian Riviera, Milan and Provence?
Yes. From Cannes or Nice we cover intercity transfers across the Italian Riviera — Sanremo, Bordighera, Portofino — and long-distance transfers to Milan (4–5 hr · from €1,350), Florence (5 hr · from €1,800), Aix-en-Provence, Avignon, and Courchevel (4 hr · from €2,100). All long-distance transfers are priced per vehicle for up to 7 passengers and arranged on enquiry.
