Nice Côte d'Azur Airport — NCE

Commercial terminal arrivals

Nice NCE has two terminals. Both handle a mix of international and European flights — there is no strict geographic split. Emirates, for example, operates from Terminal 2. The correct terminal depends on your airline and flight, not your origin.

We track your flight from departure and know your terminal automatically. You do not need to tell us — the driver is already in the right place before your bags arrive.

Meeting point Arrivals hall, past customs — name board at the barrier before the exit
Parking Parking Pro 1 or 2 depending on terminal — included in the price
Contact Direct WhatsApp to driver on the day
Nice Business Aviation — ACAM

Private jet terminal

The ACAM business aviation terminal is a separate facility at Nice Airport, used by private jet arrivals. It is not connected to the commercial terminals. If you are arriving by private aircraft, please specify this when booking — the meeting point and parking arrangements differ.

We are familiar with ACAM procedures and have served clients arriving by private jet on this route on a regular basis.

Questions

The ones people
actually ask.

How much does the transfer from Cannes to Nice Airport cost?

€130 per vehicle for up to 7 passengers, tolls included. The price confirmed at booking is the price paid — no airport fee, no fuel surcharge, no waiting-time fees within reason, no additions at the door.

How long is the drive, and how early should I leave Cannes?

Approximately 45 minutes via the A8 in normal conditions, up to 75 minutes in July and August. We generally recommend leaving Cannes at least 90 minutes before departure, 2 hours in peak summer, and we calculate the exact pickup time at booking using your flight.

Do you collect from my hotel in Cannes?

Yes — from the hotel entrance, villa gate, or yacht berth, anywhere in Cannes. We confirm the pickup point with you and the driver is in direct WhatsApp contact on the day.

Can I book the return leg from Nice Airport to Cannes?

Yes, at the same €130 rate. On the inbound leg we meet in the arrivals hall with a name board and monitor your flight from departure, so a delay adjusts the pickup automatically. We recommend booking both legs together.

What if my departure flight is early morning?

We run early-morning departures routinely. Give us the flight time at booking and we set a pickup that gets you to the terminal with comfortable margin — the A8 is clear early, so the timing is predictable.

Can you take us to the private jet terminal (ACAM)?

Yes. For private jet departures from the ACAM business aviation terminal at Nice, we collect from your Cannes address on the same fixed basis. The ACAM terminal is separate from the commercial terminals — mention the private flight when booking, as the drop-off arrangements differ.
