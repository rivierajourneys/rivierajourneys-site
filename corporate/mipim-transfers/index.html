The Event

For four days in March, Cannes becomes the capital of global real estate. MIPIM 2027 runs 15–19 March at the Palais des Festivals — twenty thousand property professionals from ninety countries, six thousand investors, four thousand C-level executives, three hundred journalists. Delegations from London, Dubai, Singapore, Paris, Berlin, Madrid. The mayors of major European capitals. Ministers of housing. The chief executives of CBRE, JLL, Savills, BNP Paribas Real Estate. La Croisette fills with black cars and folding exhibition pavilions. Every hotel lobby between the Majestic and the Martinez becomes a private meeting room. Every working hour of every day is scheduled a month in advance. Getting between meetings — reliably, on time, with six people in one vehicle — is a different problem from getting around Cannes in August. We are based in Cannes. We drive these roads year-round. During MIPIM, that is the difference between your delegation arriving at the Palais on time and explaining to the investment committee why you were not.

What We Know
Palais access and the Croisette
During MIPIM, the Croisette remains open to traffic — unlike May — but the Palais des Festivals underground car park fills by 09:00 every morning and does not empty until after the evening networking sessions end. The drop-off zone at the Croisette entrance is assigned by accreditation level. We know which entrance your exhibitor badge allows, which the investor badge prefers, and where the ministerial delegations are routed. The Rue Buttura and the Rue Félix Faure, directly behind the Palais, are the fastest approaches when the main Croisette entrance is backed up — a thirty-second walk from the car to the accreditation hall. That is where we stop when the front doors are not moving.
Back-to-back meetings
MIPIM runs on a compressed calendar: keynotes from 09:00, parallel conferences by 09:30, investor meetings in thirty-minute slots through the morning, lunch at exhibitor pavilions, afternoon sessions, networking from 17:30, dinners across town by 20:00, late receptions on the yachts at the Vieux Port or Quai Saint-Pierre. If your 11:00 keynote is in Auditorium K at the Palais and your 11:30 meeting is at the Grand Hyatt Martinez, that is one kilometre of Croisette — twelve minutes on foot in normal conditions, twenty in March when the pavement is an uninterrupted stream of exhibitors heading the other way. The difference between arriving in time and keeping the chair waiting is one car waiting exactly where you walk out, and a driver who knows that the service road behind the Carlton is usually moving when the Croisette itself is not. We know.
Evening networking
The evening receptions, the exhibitor dinners, the private yacht entertaining at the Vieux Port, the late drinks at Baoli or the private rooms at La Palme d'Or — MIPIM evenings run late. The city delegation parties at the Intercontinental Carlton have been known to go until 02:00. We wait. There is no surcharge for waiting time within your booked hours. If the evening runs past your booking, we extend at the hourly rate (€90/hr). At 01:30, when MIPIM is still awake and the taxis at the airport rank have long finished their shifts, we are outside with the engine running.
Hotels and where we pick up
The five palace hotels on the Croisette — Le Splendid, Le Majestic, The Carlton, The Martinez, JW Marriott — each have different access arrangements during MIPIM. Some allow kerbside drop-off from the Croisette side, others require the parallel-street car park entrance. The smaller hotels on Rue d'Antibes and Rue Hoche have no dedicated drop-off at all. We know each one. We also know the Mougins, Mouans-Sartoux and Grasse hotels that MIPIM attendees increasingly book when Cannes sells out by February — thirty minutes from the Palais with no traffic, forty-five at peak, and we schedule accordingly.
The Friday exodus
Every MIPIM ends the same way. Twenty thousand people trying to leave a town of seventy-five thousand, almost all of them heading to Nice Airport, largely in the space of eight hours. Cannes has around one hundred and fifty taxis and a few hundred VTCs on the road on any given day — and more than a thousand MIPIM passengers per hour need to reach the terminal. The queues at the Palais taxi rank start forming at 10:00 and do not clear until the evening. Attendees without a booked car wait forty minutes, sometimes longer, and then sit in A8 traffic that is already backed up to Mandelieu by midday. Flights get missed every year. The solution is unheroic: book your Friday departure when you book your Sunday arrival. We hold the return leg at the same rate, the driver is at your hotel at the time agreed, and you are at the terminal ninety minutes before your flight regardless of what the rest of MIPIM is doing.

Questions.
Plain answers.

What does the chauffeur service include during MIPIM?

A private Mercedes V-Class with an English-speaking driver, available by the hour. We stay with your delegation for the duration of the booking — hotel to Palais, Palais to investor pavilion, pavilion to dinner, dinner back to hotel. No rebooking between legs. The car stays, the driver waits, you walk out and we are there.

How far in advance should I book for MIPIM?

As early as possible. We take MIPIM bookings from November for the following March. The opening Sunday airport transfers, the main Wednesday and Thursday delegation days, and the closing Friday departures fill first. Last-minute requests are sometimes possible but we cannot guarantee availability. If you know your dates, even provisionally, contact us now and we will hold the booking without obligation.

Do you know the MIPIM traffic patterns?

Yes. We are based in Cannes and drive here year-round. MIPIM brings its own specific patterns — the Palais underground fills by 09:00, the Rue Buttura and the Rue Félix Faure behind the Palais are the fastest approaches when the main Croisette entrance is blocked, and the Rue d'Antibes is gridlocked through lunch hours. We know the alternative routes, the timing windows, and where to position the car so you walk thirty seconds, not fifteen minutes. This is not something you learn from a GPS.

Can you coordinate transport for a full delegation?

Yes. We coordinate multiple vehicles for investment firms, architectural practices, city delegations and national housing ministries. One point of contact, one consolidated invoice, airport arrivals handled in sequence, daily schedules synchronised across multiple executives. Tell us your schedule and group size — we handle the logistics.

What about evening events that run late?

We wait. There is no surcharge for waiting within your booked hours. If the evening runs past your booking, we extend at the hourly rate (€90/hr). Festival evenings are unpredictable by nature — we plan for that.

Can you take us to property viewings in Monaco or Saint-Tropez?

Yes. MIPIM delegations frequently combine exhibition days with off-site viewings: villa portfolios on Cap d'Antibes or Cap Ferrat, commercial sites in Monaco, development sites inland from Saint-Tropez, estates in the Provence hinterland. We hold half-day and full-day slots outside your Palais schedule for this purpose — half day from €500, full day from €900. Tell us which agent you are working with and we coordinate directly with their diary.
