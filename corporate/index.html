The Calendar

Cannes is not just a summer destination. Through the year the Palais des Festivals hosts some of the largest business and creative events in Europe — WAICF in February, Canneseries and MIPIM in spring, the Festival de Cannes in May, Cannes Lions in June, the Cannes Yachting Festival in September, TFWA and MIPCOM in autumn, MAPIC and ILTM in late autumn. Together they bring over a hundred and fifty thousand professionals to a town of seventy-five thousand residents, largely through the same airport twenty-seven kilometres away, almost entirely during the same working hours. During these weeks the city operates on a different system: hotels sell out months in advance, the two-kilometre stretch of Croisette between the Palais and the Palm Beach becomes a single slow procession, taxi queues at the Palais start before 10:00 and do not clear until the evening, the quais of the Vieux Port fill with chartered yachts, and the A8 motorway to Nice backs up to Mandelieu. We service these weeks with private chauffeur and transfers — pre-booked, pre-paid, predictable. We also handle the quieter end of the corporate calendar: retreats in the Provence hinterland above Grasse, incentives programmes along the Moyenne Corniche to Monaco, partner tours across the Cap d'Antibes peninsula, client entertaining on yachts moored three hundred metres from the exhibition hall. One point of contact, one consolidated invoice, one person who knows where everyone needs to be and when.

Why Book With Us
We are based in Cannes
Not in Nice, not in Monaco, not in a call centre with a map. Our base is in Cannes and we drive these roads year-round. We know the Palais des Festivals in February when it is quiet and in June when it is gridlocked. We know which side streets still move during MIPIM, which taxi ranks run dry by 11:00 during Lions, which hotels allow kerbside drop-off and which require the car park entrance off the parallel street. During event weeks this kind of knowledge is the difference between arriving on time and arriving late.
One point of contact for the whole week
Corporate events produce complicated logistics. An agency arrives with thirty creatives staggered across two days. A delegation has eight executives on different schedules, each with a daily programme that does not overlap with the others. The Friday departure is booked months in advance and still manages to be chaotic. We handle all of it through one person — one consolidated invoice, one WhatsApp thread, one driver roster. You send us the group itinerary and we coordinate airport arrivals, daily transport, evening pickups, Friday departures. You do not rebook between legs.
Book the return leg when you book the arrival
Every major event at the Palais ends the same way — a large number of people trying to leave Cannes at the same time, almost all of them heading to Nice Airport. Cannes has around one hundred and fifty taxis and a few hundred VTCs on the road. The A8 motorway backs up to Mandelieu by midday. The queues at the Palais taxi rank start forming at 10:00 and do not clear until evening. Attendees without a booked car wait forty minutes, then sit in traffic, and flights get missed. The solution is unheroic: book your return when you book your arrival. We hold the slot, the driver is at your hotel at the time agreed, and you are at the terminal ninety minutes before your flight regardless of what the rest of the event is doing.

Questions.
Plain answers.

What does the chauffeur service include?

A private Mercedes V-Class with an English-speaking driver, available by the hour (minimum three hours) or for the full day (up to ten hours). We stay with your team for the duration of the booking — hotel to Palais, Palais to lunch, lunch to afternoon sessions, sessions to dinner, dinner back to hotel or yacht. No rebooking between legs. The car stays, the driver waits, you walk out and we are there.

How far in advance should we book for a major event?

As early as possible. For the major events at the Palais we take bookings four to six months ahead — sometimes longer. Cannes Lions and the Festival de Cannes are typically the tightest. If you know your dates, even provisionally, contact us now and we will hold the booking without obligation. Last-minute requests are sometimes possible but we cannot guarantee availability during event weeks.

How well do you know Cannes during event weeks?

We are based in Cannes and drive these roads year-round — not just during event weeks. Each event has its own traffic pattern: MIPIM's back-streets fill differently than Lions' evening yacht runs, Festival week involves full Croisette closures that MIPIM does not. We know which side streets still move, which drop-off zones fill first, where to stop when the front entrance of any palace hotel is blocked. This is not something you learn from a GPS; it is something you learn from driving the same roads every week of the year.

Can you coordinate a full delegation or agency team?

Yes. We coordinate multiple vehicles for agencies, brand teams, investment firms, production companies, city delegations and ministerial groups. Airport arrivals staggered across two or three days, daily transport for executives on different schedules, synchronised fleet calls for gala evenings. One point of contact, one consolidated invoice, one person who knows where everyone needs to be. We have handled groups of up to forty across a five-day programme.

What about evening events and late returns?

We wait. There is no surcharge for waiting within your booked hours. If the evening runs past your booking, we extend at the hourly rate (€90/hr). Event evenings are unpredictable by nature — awards ceremonies overrun, private dinners extend, yacht parties keep going — we plan for that.

Do you handle corporate retreats and incentive programmes?

Yes. Outside the named events at the Palais, we service corporate retreats in the Provence hinterland, incentive programmes on the Riviera and Italian coast, partner tours during spouse programmes, client entertaining on yachts and at villas. Typical arrangements: a dedicated vehicle and driver for the duration, itinerary coordinated with your event manager or DMC, transfers between airport / hotel / venue / dinner / back. Pricing on enquiry — tell us the dates, the group size, the rough shape of the week, and we quote.
