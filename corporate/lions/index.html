The Festival

For five days in June, Cannes becomes the creative capital of the world. Cannes Lions 2026 runs 22–26 June at the Palais des Festivals — fifteen thousand advertising, marketing and creative professionals from ninety countries, the CMOs of every Fortune 500 brand that still believes in creative work, the executives from Meta, Google, TikTok, Adobe and LinkedIn, and the global agencies that dominate the industry: WPP, Publicis, Omnicom, IPG, Dentsu, Havas. Twenty-eight thousand award entries judged across the week. Four evening ceremonies for the Lions. Along the Croisette, brand activations — the Adobe Creative Loft, the Carlton Beach installations, the Meta cabanas, the TikTok Beach. At the Vieux Port and Port Pierre Canto, yachts chartered for client entertaining, some booked two years in advance. The Rue d'Antibes, the Gutter Bar, the Martinez rooftop, the Carlton terrace. Lions runs on a schedule of Palais by day, beach by afternoon, rooftop by evening, yacht by night, and back to the hotel by 04:00 — if you leave before then. Getting between them reliably, at the right times, with your team intact, requires someone who knows the city not just in June but year-round. We do. During Cannes Lions, that is the difference between the agency arriving together for the Titanium ceremony and three of you still stuck in traffic on the Boulevard de la Croisette.

What We Know
The Croisette during Lions
Cannes Lions has a different traffic pattern from the Film Festival. The Croisette stays open, but brand activations — Adobe Creative Loft, Meta Beach, TikTok Cabanas, Spotify House, Shutterstock Beach — block entire stretches of frontage between the Carlton and the Majestic. The Palais underground fills early. The Rue d'Antibes becomes one continuous queue by 10:30. We know which back streets still move, which kerbsides allow thirty-second drop-offs without attracting the local police, and which approaches to the Carlton Beach entrance are closed that morning and which are open. We route around the brand build-outs, not through them.
The Lions calendar
Cannes Lions runs on a specific rhythm: Palais seminars from 09:00, networking at the brand activations by 11:00, client lunches at beach cabanas or on yachts from 12:30, afternoon sessions and Academy talks, the daily Awards ceremonies from 19:00 to 21:30 (Grand Prix and Titanium Friday), private agency dinners from 22:00, and rooftop/beach parties running past 02:00. If your 19:30 seat at the Palais is for the Film ceremony and your 21:00 client dinner is at Hotel Martinez, the difference between making both with dignity intact and arriving at dinner sweaty and thirty minutes late is one driver who knows the Rue Hoche shortcut to la Voie Rapide, which bypasses the Croisette entirely. We do.
Lions evenings run late
Cannes Lions evenings are the defining part of the week. The awards at 19:30, private dinners at Le Park 45 or Villa Archange from 22:00, after-parties at Baoli or the Carlton Beach, late rooftop sessions at Martinez or 3.14, and then the yacht afterparties that end when dawn does. We wait. There is no surcharge for waiting time within your booked hours. If the evening runs past your booking, we extend at the hourly rate (€90/hr). At 03:30 on Wednesday, when the Lions crowd is still spilling out of Baoli onto the Croisette and every taxi rank is empty, we are outside with the engine running. This is our busiest week of the year; we plan for it.
Hotels, yachts, villas
Cannes Lions fills every hotel on the Croisette — Carlton, Martinez, Majestic, Splendid, JW Marriott — months before the week begins. Overflow goes to the Grand, the Grays, and inland to Mougins and Mouans-Sartoux. The largest agencies charter entire yachts at the Vieux Port and Port Pierre Canto; some brand teams rent villas on Cap d'Antibes and Cap Ferrat for the week. We know the access points and parking situation at every palace hotel. We know the yacht quays, the berth numbers and which passerelles accept vehicle drop-offs. We know the villa gate codes (when given), the estate managers, the discreet back entrances. We schedule around each without being told the logistics twice.

Questions.
Plain answers.

What does the chauffeur service include during Cannes Lions?

A private Mercedes V-Class with an English-speaking driver, available by the hour. We stay with your team for the duration of the booking — hotel to Palais, Palais to beach cabana, cabana to yacht reception, yacht to awards, awards to dinner, dinner to rooftop, rooftop to hotel. No rebooking between legs. The car stays, the driver waits, you walk out and we are there.

How far in advance should I book for Cannes Lions?

As early as possible. Cannes Lions is our busiest week of the year — we take bookings from December for the following June. The Sunday and Monday airport arrivals, the awards evenings (Tuesday through Friday), and the Saturday departures fill first. Last-minute requests are sometimes possible but we cannot guarantee availability. If you know your dates, even provisionally, contact us now and we will hold the booking without obligation.

Do you know the Cannes Lions traffic patterns?

Yes. We are based in Cannes and drive here year-round. Cannes Lions brings its own patterns — the Carlton Beach drop-off jams around midday, the Vieux Port yacht quays move one-way after 17:00, the Rue d'Antibes is one continuous queue from 10:30, and the Rue Hoche is the only reliable way to reach the Martinez rooftop after dark. We know the back routes, the timing windows, and the private access half the delegates do not know exists. This is not something you learn from a GPS.

Can you coordinate transport for a full delegation?

Yes. We coordinate multiple vehicles for investment firms, architectural practices, city delegations and national housing ministries. One point of contact, one consolidated invoice, airport arrivals handled in sequence, daily schedules synchronised across multiple executives. Tell us your schedule and group size — we handle the logistics.

What about evening events that run late?

We wait. There is no surcharge for waiting within your booked hours. If the evening runs past your booking, we extend at the hourly rate (€90/hr). Festival evenings are unpredictable by nature — we plan for that.

Can you handle yacht and villa transfers during Lions?

Yes. Many Cannes Lions attendees stay on yachts at the Vieux Port and Port Pierre Canto, or rent villas on Cap d'Antibes, Cap Ferrat, Mougins. The transport between yacht-Palais-beach cabana-dinner-yacht is often the busiest service during the week. We know the berth numbers, the villa gate codes (when given), the estate managers. We book dedicated vehicles for the duration, or handle point-to-point transfers as required. Discretion as standard.
