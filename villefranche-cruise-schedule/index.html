Schedule

Villefranche-sur-Mer is the main cruise tender port for Nice and one of the busiest on the French Riviera. Ships anchor in one of the deepest natural harbours in the Mediterranean, and tenders bring passengers ashore to quai de l’Amiral Courbet, in the old village itself. From there, Nice is fifteen minutes by car. Monaco and Eze are twenty minutes east. Cannes, Antibes and Saint-Paul-de-Vence are forty-five minutes west. In 2026, 144 ships are scheduled to call at Villefranche, from luxury yachts carrying 112 guests to Norwegian Epic with over five thousand passengers. This is the complete schedule.

Port guide.
What you need to know.

Is Villefranche a tender port?

Yes. Ships anchor in the bay and tender boats bring passengers ashore to quai de l’Amiral Courbet, in the old village. The tender ride takes 10 to 15 minutes. We meet you at the tender landing with a name board.

Where do you meet us?

At quai de l’Amiral Courbet \u2014 the tender landing pier in Villefranche. We are there before your tender docks. The car is thirty seconds from the pier.

How long do we have ashore?

It depends on your ship. Most Villefranche calls give 7 to 10 hours ashore. Tell us your ship name and we confirm the shore window and recommend an itinerary.

What can we see from Villefranche?

Nice is 15 minutes away. Monaco and Eze are 20 minutes east. Cannes, Antibes and Saint-Paul-de-Vence are 45 minutes west. Grasse and the Provence hills are about an hour.

Can the tender be delayed?

Yes. Tender delays at Villefranche are common, especially when multiple ships are in port. We wait at no extra charge. If your shore time is reduced, we shorten the itinerary.

How many ships call at Villefranche in 2026?

There are 144 scheduled port calls in 2026, from 38 different ships operated by 20 cruise lines. The busiest months are May, June and September.
