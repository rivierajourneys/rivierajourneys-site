Two cruise ports serve the French Riviera. Villefranche-sur-Mer — a tender port in one of the deepest natural harbours in the Mediterranean — receives the majority of ships calling at Nice. Cannes — where tenders bring you ashore at the old port near boulevard Jean Hibert. From either port, the same destinations are within reach: Nice, Eze, Monaco, Antibes, Saint-Paul-de-Vence, Grasse. The difference is driving time and the order of the stops. We design the day around your ship's schedule. €800 per vehicle, up to seven passengers, Mercedes V-Class. Back before the tender.

Questions. Plain answers.

What is a shore excursion?

A private day tour that starts and ends at your cruise ship's port of call. We meet you at the tender dock or harbour, drive you to the highlights of the French Riviera in a Mercedes V-Class, and return you before your ship departs. No group. No strangers. Your schedule, your route.

Which cruise ports do you serve?

Two ports: Villefranche-sur-Mer and Cannes. Villefranche is a tender port — your ship anchors in the bay and a small boat brings you to shore. Cannes is a direct dock — ships tie up at boulevard Jean Hibert and you walk off. Both ports access the same destinations along the Côte d'Azur.

How much does a private shore excursion cost?

€800 per vehicle for up to 7 passengers. Fixed price regardless of group size. The vehicle is a Mercedes V-Class. Fuel, parking, tolls, and waiting time are included. No hidden costs.

Will we be back before the ship departs?

Always. We know the tender schedule, we track your ship, and we plan the day to return you with comfortable margin. If tender delays occur — and they often do at Villefranche — we wait at no extra charge. We have never missed a ship.

Can we customise the route?

Entirely. Every route we list is a suggestion, not a schedule. Skip Monaco and spend longer in Eze. Add a stop at Cap Ferrat. Visit the Matisse museum instead of the market. We design the day around what you actually want to see. Tell us your preferences when booking and we adjust accordingly.
