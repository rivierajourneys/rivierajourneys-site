Villefranche-sur-Mer is a tender port. Ships anchor in the bay, one of the deepest natural harbours in the Mediterranean, and tenders bring you ashore to quai de l’Amiral Courbet in the centre of the town. We are waiting at the landing with a name board. The geography here is the most compact on the coast: Nice is fifteen minutes west, the Moyenne Corniche and Eze begin ten minutes east, and Monaco is about twenty-five. €800 per vehicle, up to seven passengers, Mercedes V-Class. The day is built around your tender window rather than around a fixed departure.

Your day ashore

What fits, and when

The earlier you land, the more the day holds, and the hour of the last tender decides the rest. This is true of every cruise call rather than of Villefranche in particular — though the short transit times here buy you back an hour that Cannes does not.

Ashore by 10:00
All three routes fit at full length. Nice, Eze and Monaco with unhurried time in each, or west to Cannes, Antibes and Saint-Paul-de-Vence, or inland to Grasse and Gourdon.
Ashore from 11:00
The eastern route still works, because almost none of the day is spent in transit. The westward route to Cannes, Antibes and Saint-Paul is the one that begins to run short: it opens with the motorway past the airport, and an hour goes before the first stop.
The return
We plan to have you back at quai de l’Amiral Courbet thirty to forty-five minutes before the last tender. If the morning runs late, we shorten a stop rather than that margin.

Questions. Plain answers.

Does a cruise ship dock in Villefranche, or do you tender ashore?

Villefranche-sur-Mer is a tender port. Ships anchor in the bay, one of the deepest natural harbours in the Mediterranean, and tenders bring you ashore to quai de l’Amiral Courbet in the centre of the town.

Where do you meet us in Villefranche?

At quai de l’Amiral Courbet, where the tenders land. We are waiting with a name board before your tender comes in, and the vehicle is a short walk from the quay.

How much does a private shore excursion from Villefranche cost?

€800 per vehicle for up to 7 passengers. The price is fixed regardless of group size. The vehicle is a Mercedes V-Class. Fuel, parking, tolls and waiting time are included.

How much can we realistically see in the time we have ashore?

The geography works in your favour here. Nice is fifteen minutes west and Monaco about twenty-five minutes east, so the eastern route holds together even on a shorter day. Ashore by ten, all three routes fit at full length. From eleven, the eastern route still works; the westward route to Cannes, Antibes and Saint-Paul is the one that begins to run short, since it opens with the motorway past the airport.

What happens if the tender runs late?

We wait, at no extra charge. Tender delays are a normal part of an anchorage port and we plan for them. We stay in WhatsApp contact through the day and shorten a stop rather than the return margin if the morning runs late.

Will we be back before the last tender?

Yes. We plan the return for thirty to forty-five minutes before the last tender back to the ship.
